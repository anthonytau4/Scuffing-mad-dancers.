ABOUT SCUFFING MAD DANCERS

A lively, local night out with proper heart.

Scuffing Mad Dancers brings people together for upbeat line dancing in a warm, welcoming space at Tawa Scout Hall. Whether you are stepping onto the floor for the first time or polishing what you already know, there is room for you here.

Beginner Friendly

Clear teaching, steady progress, and no pressure.

Good Music

A lively mix that keeps the room moving all night.

Community Feel

Supportive people, easy smiles, and plenty of fun.

Room To Improve

Build confidence and take on more as you go.
